Our Products

Sustainable Use of Mangrove ecosystem for Livelihood and Conservation

Our Products

Kadzuhoni Mangrove Conservation works with local communities to produce and market a variety of mangrove, aquaculture and marine ecosystems-based products. By promoting sustainable harvesting and production techniques, we ensure that these products benefit both the environment and the livelihoods of our people.

These products include:

  • Mangrove seedlings
  • Mangrove Honey
  • Fish
  • Crabs
  • Lobsters
  • Brine shrimp (Artemia)

Our Main Products

Mangrove Honey

Produced from our beekeeping efforts, mangrove honey is rich in flavor and nutrients, harvested sustainably from local hives.

Mangrove seedlings

We established mangrove nurseries with all 9-mangrove species available in Kenya using biodegradable potting tubes. Our seedlings are healthy.

Artemia (Brine shrimp)

We produce Artemia biomass and cysts from our aquaculture farm. Artemia are rich in protein (about 60%) and also have other nutrients like Carbohydrates and Fat.

Value-Added Mangrove Products

Our team is also developing value-added mangrove products, including processed natural dye from mangrove by-products. These items provide additional income streams for local families, supporting economic growth without compromising the mangrove ecosystem.

These innovations ensure that every part of the mangrove forest is utilized sustainably, adding value to our conservation and livelihood goals.

Impact on the Community

Our mangrove product initiatives have had a significant impact on the local community by providing sustainable livelihoods while promoting conservation.

Increased Incomes

Our sustainable mangrove products have improved household incomes by creating marketable goods from natural resources.

Women Empowerment

Women in the community play a central role in the production of crafts, enhancing their economic independence and status.

Environmental Awareness

Through environmental friendly practices, community members have gained a deeper appreciation of the importance of conserving mangrove ecosystem and its related associates.
